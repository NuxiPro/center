## What is NuxiPro?

NuxiPro is a minimalist, lightweight task manager with no unnecessary features that clutter your workspace. Its core purpose is to create a **self-cleaning workspace** — tasks that reach "Done" are archived automatically.

Most Kanban tools make it easy to add tasks and features, but none keep your workspace clean and clutter-free. NuxiPro solves this problem.

## The Problem

Today's tools help us add tasks, but none help us remove them. Without automatic cleanup, the "Done" column becomes a graveyard of cards. We waste precious time figuring out how to archive and cleaning up manually, instead of moving our project forward.

I built NuxiPro to offer a simple, minimalist Kanban that's ready to use. As soon as a task slides into the "Done" column, it's instantly archived in the background. Minimal configuration, zero complexity, and your board stays clean from day one.

## How it works

- **Instant archiving (demo):** Tasks archive instantly when moved to Done. Data stays in your browser (localStorage).
- **Configurable archiving (cloud):** Choose your archiving mode — instant or time-based (X days). Persistent server storage.

## Key Features

- **Zero friction** — Manual archiving disappears completely
- **Zero visual clutter** — No more completed tasks polluting your workspace
- **Simple tracking** — Visualize your progress and daily wins, effortlessly
- **No useless features** — Focus on what matters without being overwhelmed
- **Quick start** — A tool ready to use, simple day-to-day

## NuxiPro Cloud — Coming Soon

The Cloud version of NuxiPro ([app.nuxipro.com](https://app.nuxipro.com)) is currently in development.

### What NuxiPro Cloud will offer

- **Secure data storage** — Your data is stored in a database, not in your browser's localStorage
- **Data sovereignty** — You own your data, always
- **Multi-project support** — Each project appears as a line in the sidebar with its own name
- **Auto-archiving of completed tasks** — Finished tasks are automatically moved to the archive
- **Minimalist tools** — No useless features you'll never use
- **Dedicated account** — One account per user
- **Workspaces** — Switch between workspaces without losing time
- **Low configuration** — Choose your archiving mode at setup, change it anytime

### How archiving works in Cloud

Two modes to choose from:

- **Instant** — Task archives immediately when moved to "Done"
- **Time-based** — Task archives after X days (configurable)

Your tasks are visually separated from your workspace. Completed tasks move to a dedicated archive zone without being loaded when you open your project — avoiding performance issues.

Your workspace stays clean, free of visual clutter from completed tasks. You can always find your archived tasks in the archive zone without ever losing them.

### Why the demo is not made for real projects

The demo is designed for demonstration only. If you store sensitive data, you may lose it permanently because storage is done in your browser (localStorage). The Cloud version solves this with persistent server-side storage.
