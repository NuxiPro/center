## What is NuxiPro?

NuxiPro is a minimalist task manager with no unnecessary features you won't use — an alternative to Trello without its complexity.

Its main goal is to create a workspace that cleans itself up, so it never becomes visually overloaded with completed tasks by archiving them automatically.

## The Problem It Solves

Most current tools help you add tasks easily, but none help you keep your workspace properly clean without complicated configurations.

If you don't automate the cleaning of the "Done" column, it becomes a graveyard of completed tasks. On top of that, we're forced to clean our workspace by hand, and we waste a huge amount of time figuring out how to clean manually instead of moving forward with our project.

In short, the tool becomes the project itself through all the effort of managing the tool.

## How It Works

You move your tasks to the Done column, and the system moves your tasks to the dedicated archive zone so you never lose your data and keep a record of your completed work.

## Key Features

- **Zero visual clutter** — No more completed tasks polluting your workspace
- **Simple tracking** — Visualize your progress and daily wins
- **No unnecessary features** — Focus on the essentials without being overwhelmed
- **Quick start** — A tool ready to use, effective day-to-day

## NuxiPro Cloud — Coming Soon

The Cloud version of NuxiPro ([app.nuxipro.com](https://app.nuxipro.com)) is currently in development.

### What NuxiPro Cloud Will Offer

- **Secure data storage** — Your data is stored in a database, not in the browser's localStorage
- **Data sovereignty** — You remain the owner of your data, and it will be stored with UpCloud in Finland
- **Multi-project support** — Each project appears as a line in the sidebar with its own name
- **Automatic archiving of completed tasks** — Completed tasks are automatically moved to the archive zone without visually cluttering
- **Minimalist tools** — No unnecessary features you'll never use
- **Dedicated account**
- **Workspaces** — Switch between your workspaces without wasting time
- **Light configuration** — Choose your archiving mode at setup, change it anytime

### How Archiving Works in Cloud

Two modes to choose from:

- **Instant** — The task archives immediately when moved to "Done"
- **Time-delayed** — The task archives after X days (configurable)

Your tasks are visually separated from your workspace. Completed tasks move to a dedicated archive zone without being loaded when you open your project — avoiding performance issues.

Your dashboard stays visually clean, free of visual clutter from completed tasks. You can always find your archived tasks in the archive zone without ever losing them.

### Why the Demo Isn't Made for Real Projects

The demo is designed solely for demonstration purposes. If you store sensitive data there, you risk losing it permanently because storage happens in your browser (localStorage). The Cloud version solves this with persistent server-side storage.
